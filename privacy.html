Privacy Policy

Readable Dev Tools processes all data locally in your browser.

No data leaves your browser.

No logs, JSON, stack traces, diffs, or source code are uploaded to any server.

No API Calls No Cookies No localStorage No sessionStorage No Tracking

What We Do Not Use

We do not upload pasted content to a server.

We do not store pasted content in browser storage.

We do not use cookies or analytics tracking.

All formatting runs in browser memory and disappears when the page is closed.
